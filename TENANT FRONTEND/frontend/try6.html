Settings & Troubleshooting

Platform Troubleshooting
Cannot access my documents

If you're having trouble accessing your documents, try clearing your browser cache or try using a different browser. If the issue persists, contact support.

Payment not showing in history

Payments may take up to 24 hours to appear in your payment history. If your payment doesn't appear after this time, check with your bank and then contact support.

Maintenance request not submitted

Ensure you've filled all required fields and have a stable internet connection. Try submitting again. If the problem continues, try using a different device.

Unable to upload files

Check that your file is in PDF or image format and doesn't exceed 10MB in size. If you continue to have issues, try compressing the file or contact support.
